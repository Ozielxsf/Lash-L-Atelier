# Accessibility Statement

**Lash L’Atelier**
Effective date: September 25, 2026

Lash L’Atelier is committed to ensuring that our website at lashlatelier.com (the "Site") is
accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We believe every visitor deserves
a clear, usable experience, and we work to make our Site as inclusive as we reasonably can.

The Site was designed and built on our behalf by **Wallink Systems** with accessibility as a
core part of the build.

---

## Our commitment

We aim to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA**,
published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines are the internationally
recognized standard for making web content more accessible, and they are the benchmark
generally referenced in connection with the **Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)**. We
also strive to follow the applicable requirements of the ADA and other relevant accessibility
laws.

## Measures we take

To support accessibility, the Site is built with practices that include:

- **Semantic, well-structured HTML** so content is meaningful to assistive technologies such
  as screen readers.
- **Keyboard navigability** — interactive elements can be reached and operated without a
  mouse, with visible focus indicators.
- **Text alternatives** — meaningful images include descriptive alternative ("alt") text.
- **Sufficient color contrast** between text and background to aid readability.
- **Responsive, resizable layouts** that adapt to different screen sizes and support browser
  zoom and text resizing.
- **Descriptive links, labels, and headings** that make the page structure clear.
- **Forms with associated labels** and clear error messaging.

## Conformance status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines define requirements at three levels — A, AA, and AAA.
This Site is designed to be **substantially conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level AA**, meaning it
meets the majority of those requirements. We assess accessibility using a combination of
automated tools and manual review, including keyboard and screen-reader checks, as part of
building and maintaining the Site.

## Ongoing effort

Accessibility is an ongoing process, not a one-time task. We review accessibility as part of
building and maintaining the Site and work to address issues as they are identified. As
content, features, and standards evolve, we continue to make reasonable improvements.

## Known limitations and third-party content

Despite our best efforts, some parts of the Site may not yet be fully accessible, and portions
of the Site provided by third parties (such as embedded maps, social-media feeds, payment
widgets, or other external tools) may be outside our direct control and may not fully conform
to WCAG 2.1 AA. We work to choose accessible third-party services where possible and to
provide accessible alternatives where practical.

We are committed to making a good-faith, reasonable effort toward accessibility. This
statement reflects that commitment and is not a warranty or guarantee that the Site meets
every accessibility requirement at all times.

## Compatibility

The Site is designed to be compatible with current versions of major browsers and with common
assistive technologies. If you use an older browser or assistive technology, some features may
not work as intended, and we recommend keeping your software up to date for the best
experience.
